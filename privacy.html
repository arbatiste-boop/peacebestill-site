Privacy Policy

Peace Be Still: Spiritual Well-Being & Calm · Effective October 16, 2026

The short version: Peace Be Still has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

What the app stores on your device

The following is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone or computer:

  • Mood check-ins: the mood you pick, any feeling tags, and any note you write.
  • The days you were active (used for the streak shown on the home screen).
  • Scripture verses you have requested, and the verses you were recently shown.
  • Coloring progress, favorites, and any SVG files you import.
  • Your settings: your name and email if you type them into Profile, your reminder time, and any template library address you enter.

This information is never sent to the developer. If you delete the app, it is removed with it.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not use analytics or advertising tools, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Network connections the app can make

The app works fully offline. It makes a network request only in these optional cases:

  • Coloring template library. If you enter a library web address in Profile, the app downloads the catalog and template files from that address. The server you chose can see your IP address, like any website. No library address is set by default.
  • Adding a verse (Admin Panel). If you look up a new Bible verse, the app sends only the verse reference (for example "Psalm 91:1") to bible-api.com, a free third-party service, which can see your IP address. Verse text built into the app does not need any request.
  • Crisis links. Buttons such as "Call 988" or "Text 988" hand off to your phone's own calling and messaging apps. Those services are run by your carrier and by the services you contact, not by this app.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your permission and schedules a notification on your device. It is created and delivered locally. No notification service of ours is involved, and you can turn it off at any time in Profile or in your device Settings.

Your choices and control

  • Export: Profile → Your data → Export my data saves a copy of your check-ins, activity days and scripture history.
  • Delete: Profile → Your data → Delete all my data removes everything the app has stored. Deleting the app does the same.

Because we hold none of your data, we cannot look up, correct or delete it for you. You control it directly in the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects nothing, there is no child data to remove.

Not medical advice; if you need help now

Peace Be Still is a self-care and well-being tool. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition and is not a substitute for professional care. If you may be in danger or are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US), text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line), or call 911. The "Need help now?" button inside the app shows these options.

Scripture text

Bible verses are from the King James Version, which is in the public domain.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, for example by adding an optional online feature, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before the new version is released.
